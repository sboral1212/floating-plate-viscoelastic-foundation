--- title: Dynamic Response of a Floating Elastic Plate on a Viscoelastic Foundation description: Supporting computational and neural-network files for the published Applied Mathematics and Computation article on a floating elastic plate under a moving periodic load. --- # Dynamic Response of a Floating Elastic Plate on a Viscoelastic Foundation ## Analytical and Neural Modelling Framework This website provides the supporting computational and machine-learning files associated with the article: **Boral, S. (2026). Dynamic response of a floating elastic plate supported on a viscoelastic foundation under moving periodic load: Analytical and neural modelling.** *Applied Mathematics and Computation*, **516**, 129872. [View the published article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.amc.2025.129872) [View the GitHub repository](https://github.com/sboral1212/floating-plate-viscoelastic-foundation) ## Overview The work investigates the hydroelastic response of a floating elastic plate supported by a viscoelastic foundation and subjected to a moving periodic load. It includes analytical modelling, resonance analysis and neural-network surrogate modelling. ## Repository contents - Neural-network implementation - Supporting scripts and datasets - Example configurations - Files required to reproduce the reported neural-network results
